What is this?

Visualizing Visuals is an art church — a space where ideas become interactive experiences. Part thrift-store aesthetic, part generative code, part plein-air field study. The work spans oil on canvas, digital art, p5.js sketches, and whatever medium best holds the particular truth being pursued.

Every piece here started as a feeling someone wanted to see externalized. A mood. A pattern. A question with no words yet. That's what gets made visible.

Who depoyed this?

A man who dropped his IT degree to live Wu Wei. A satori-lived wanderer who drives a 2021 Nissan Kicks he calls his mobile monastery, wears a homemade muslin robe dyed with foraged natural materials, and carries a fishing bobber as the central symbol of his spiritual practice.

Clean from nicotine and THC — because satori is a better high. Living in North Alabama, Hustling to make pivot shifts work. Father of a three-month-old boy. Steadily becoming.

The Bobber

The fishing bobber is the central symbol — the still point resting in moving water. Something about waiting without forcing. About letting the current do the work while you remain perfectly upright, perfectly patient, perfectly present.

Tao meets art. Wu Wei meets generative code. That's the whole thing.
